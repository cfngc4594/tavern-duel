Two independent stages now use same-draw-order RGB/mask targets plus a blend-disabled merge: title glyph RTT (then original name AA curve shader), and complete 3D card view. For SrcAlpha/OneMinusSrcAlpha passes the RGB numerical mask emits (a,a,a,a), giving R=a*a+dstR*(1-a). Merge takes original color.rgb and mask.r, never multiplies color by alpha again. For the six opaque One/Zero materials mask writes the recovered original fragment alpha directly, NOT a*a and NOT assumed1.

Opaque equations/material data are under ../source_materials; original alpha inputs include main/second textures, original UV0/UV1, normalized vertex color (if present), and material uniforms.
