# v10 surface and indicator provenance

Official visual reference: https://blizzard.gamespress.com/BlizzCon-2013-Hearthstone-Press-Kit
Local research source: Classic-PriestVsHunter.mp4, 1280×720, 30000/1001 fps.
Observed intervals: 289.0–292.5 s (taunt), 435.0–436.5 s (normal).

The taunt silhouette is a broad shallow bowed top, nearly vertical upper sides and a tapered unlit bottom. The reference rim has directional metallic highlights and curved surfaces, rather than flat concentric polygons. The green edge has a lighter yellow-lime inner crest, saturated body and a darker diffuse outer envelope; local colors differ around the silhouette. The source is lossy and partly overlapped by stars/other effects, so original shaders and exact temporal behavior are not recovered.

All four sculpted_*_v10.png assets are original code-generated relief/color assets, generated by tools/build_sculpted_frames.py. The metal RGB is shaded from height-field surface normals, broad directional illumination and specular reflections. The shield height field has a domed central surface, rolled edge, recessed groove and sloped shoulder folds. The oval frame has a rounded toroidal section with inset fine grooves. Green colors are a signed-distance ramp with static local hue differences; its actual bottom alpha fades out at badge height. The hidden rear rim gives thickness without changing gameplay geometry.

Attack and health badges are original curved procedural shapes in scripts/game.gd, with interpolated directional lighting. Attack is a gold disc with two diagonal metal spurs; health is an upward-pointed ruby droplet. The numerical display uses licensed Patua One (licenses/STAT-NUMERALS-SOURCE.txt), with dark outlines and warm relief shading. It is not the original Hearthstone Belwe font.

Artwork, material detail and numeral glyphs still differ from the original. No claim of exact reconstruction is made. Audio files and playback mapping are unchanged.
