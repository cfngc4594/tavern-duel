# v14 actual-render scope

The accompanying comparison uses an actual Godot OpenGL Compatibility recording made on 2026-10-16 at 15:40 UTC. It shows independently rendered live cards, minions, mana and interaction, over the documented static board reconstruction. It is not video playback in the game.

After that recording the final source received measured hand fan/hover corrections, overlap hit testing, smaller hero health numbers, a wider deck edge, seven source-history fixture icons, and a source-matched final Coin pointer. These changes are headless-tested but not visually re-recorded. The final GUI launch was blocked by an execution safety check. Do not describe this preview as pixel-identical to the final ZIP.

Reference: official BlizzCon 2013 Classic-PriestVsHunter.mp4, 137.5–141.5 seconds. Both full 16:9 viewports are scaled equally to 960×540. MovieMaker output is trimmed by 60 warmup frames (2 seconds). The comparison contains normal speed followed by half speed; source mouse-down/up timing is inferred within approximately one frame.

Strict 1:1 remains unachieved: no authenticated 2013 Pandaria mesh and animation set, no original material/particle shader stack, no authenticated 2013 font package or exact body font. Hidden board pixels are generated restoration; 2016 Joust UI and present website Belwe are version-qualified sources, not authenticated 2013 extraction. Remaining card-face placement, body typography, turn-button contour, mana treatment and per-frame glow/particle behavior differ. Corner animation and full Priest/Hunter gameplay are outside this bounded fixture. The default playable practice pool is the supported eleven-card Mage subset, not all Hearthstone content.
